01 / OVERVIEW

About the project

More than just messaging. A concept built around connection.

✦

What is Gaze?

Gaze is a simple chatting and messaging application concept focused on connecting people through convenient digital communication.

The project explores how a user-friendly messaging platform can help people communicate, share ideas, and maintain meaningful connections.

◎

Our Mission

To make everyday communication more accessible, straightforward, and enjoyable.

⌘

Project Focus

Messaging, user experience, digital communication, and building connections.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

The problem we want to solve.

?

Communication should feel effortless.

Digital communication can become complicated when applications are difficult to navigate or do not provide a comfortable user experience.

Gaze explores a simpler approach: creating a messaging experience where connecting with others feels natural and convenient.
