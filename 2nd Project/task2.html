Why I Love Reading Books and Watching Movies

By Vishal · September 17, 2026

Reading books is one of my favourite hobbies because they help me explore new ideas, stories, and different perspectives. Every book I read gives me something new to learn and think about.

I also enjoy watching movies, especially mystery and action stories. A good movie can be exciting and memorable, and it can also help me understand different cultures and ways of thinking.
