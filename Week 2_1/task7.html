How to Make Mo:Mo

Ingredients

  • 250g ground meat
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper
  • Water

Steps

  1. Mix the flour with water and make a soft dough
  2. Mix the meat, onion, garlic, salt, and pepper in a bowl
  3. Roll the dough into small, thin circles
  4. Put a spoonful of filling in each wrapper and fold the edges
  5. Steam the momos for 10–15 minutes
  6. Serve hot with spicy Mo:Mo chutney
